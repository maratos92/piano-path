# Piano Path privacy policy draft

This describes version 0.5.0-dev.1. Before store publication, the owner must add a public contact and publish this policy at a stable URL.

Piano Path runs offline and has no account, advertising or analytics SDK. It does not record microphone audio. It receives MIDI pitch, velocity, note release, controller and timestamp events from a Bluetooth or USB instrument, or touch input. Bluetooth scanning is used to find MIDI devices. On Android 8–11 the system also requires Location permission for BLE discovery; the app does not request geographic coordinates or build location histories.

Imported scores, selected passages, settings, practice summaries and recorded note events are stored in private app storage. A visual review retains the score used for that performance. The app does not upload or share these records. Android backup is disabled. Uninstalling the app or clearing its Android app storage deletes local scores, takes and history. Externally stored original files remain under the user's control.

Settings offers a voluntary-support link to Ko-fi, which opens in the external browser. Piano Path has no payment SDK or form and does not receive payment credentials. Ko-fi and its payment processors handle payment and associated personal data under their own privacy terms. App features remain free regardless of support. Google Play and Android may separately process installation, device and diagnostic data under their own policies.

Optional daily reminders use Android notifications and inexact local alarms. They are disabled until enabled by the user; Android 13+ notification permission is requested only then. Reminder text does not include score titles. Local progress, goals and dated ear-training outcomes are kept on-device. Free-play recordings retain raw MIDI events and checkpoint drafts; the app never captures microphone audio. No cloud provider or new account system has been introduced.
