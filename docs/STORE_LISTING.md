**App name:** Piano Path

**Short description:** Offline piano practice with MIDI, sheet music and visual note feedback.

**Full description:**

Make room for music with Piano Path, a free, open-source Android piano practice app.

Connect a compatible digital piano through Bluetooth LE MIDI or class-compliant USB MIDI. Practise without an instrument using the touch keyboard, or listen to a score through the built-in acoustic piano samples. No microphone is needed.

Import your own MusicXML or MXL piano scores. Read traditional notation or follow falling notes aligned with the full 88-key keyboard. Slow the tempo, choose either hand, select a passage and loop it with a count-in and metronome.

See separate pitch and rhythm results, including missing and extra notes and onset/release deviations. Overlay your played notes and key-hold traces on the score, then reopen recorded takes from local history. Wait mode advances when you complete each note or chord.

Build technique with scales and arpeggios, recommended fingerings for common keys, and generated sight-reading exercises. MIDI cannot measure which physical finger or hand you used. The offline library contains 50 original studies; you can import legally obtained repertoire editions.

Piano Path works offline after installation. It has no account, advertising or analytics. Imported scores and practice records stay on your device. If a voluntary-support link is included, it opens an external payment provider; app features remain free.

Compatibility depends on the instrument and Android device. Bluetooth audio alone is not Bluetooth MIDI. USB requires a data cable and compatible host adapter. Assessment follows written score order at the selected tempo; repeats and expressive tempo maps are not unfolded automatically.
